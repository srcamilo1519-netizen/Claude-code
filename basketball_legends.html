Decade by Decade

Timeline of Eras

Each era emerges with depth as you scroll the spine of NBA history.

1980s

The Showtime Era

Magic's Lakers and Bird's Celtics waged the rivalry that saved the league. Fast breaks, no-look passes, prime-time basketball.

1990s

The Jordan Era

Two Bulls three-peats and global superstardom. Air Jordan made basketball the world's game.

2000s

Kobe & Shaq · Spurs Dynasty

A three-peat in LA, the relentless Spurs, and the Mamba Mentality taking root.

2010s

The LeBron Decade

Eight straight Finals, the Decision, the comeback — King James bent the era to his will.

2015–2022

The Splash Revolution

Golden State weaponized the three-point line. Curry changed how the game is played at every level.

2020s

The Modern Era

Wembanyama, LaMelo, Tatum and SGA usher in a positionless, global, sky-high new chapter.
